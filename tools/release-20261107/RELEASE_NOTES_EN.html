Jack-o-nine-tails v2.4.1 · Cumulative Update 3

Easy mode shows you what kind of slave a customer wants before you deliver. Dialogue varies as bids go back and forth, and the new Special Cooking order can raise meal quality by one grade.

For the existing 2.4.1 game. This package includes earlier updates and the WORKSHOP repair. The notes below cover changes since Update 2 r4.

Installation guide · 한국어

Improvements and Changes

Request guidance for new players

In Easy mode, open Objectives → Special Customer Requests and select a request. The bottom of the details shows the required rank, skills, traits, and other conditions for the slave the customer wants. You can see whom to bring and what still needs training.

Auction dialogue that changes as bidding continues

When bidders repeated the same lines every time they raised the price, auctions felt flat. In guild sale auctions, a returning bidder now varies what they say. The auction also has revised dialogue from consignment through the winning bid.

In market purchase auctions, Jappa has different lines for introducing lots, reacting to bids, and announcing the winner. His offers and payout dialogue are also revised for direct purchases and consignment outside the auction.

Gameplay and Balance

  • Agrabah’s fresh fruit and wine come in bundles for several meals. You can pay for fruit in sparks if you are short on gold.
  • Workaholic and Lethargic affect work, training, and rest differently. Older traits in existing saves follow the updated rules.
  • All-Rounder is an upgrade to Versatile. Guild auction prices account for both traits so training across a range of skills can pay off.
  • New randomly generated characters are less likely to get Slave Mentality. Characters who already have it keep it.
  • The customer’s preferences can affect the reward for the Biker request.

Quality of Life and Interface

  • Special Customer Requests uses separate pages when the list grows.
  • The assistant’s Get Ingredients panel groups automatic purchases in one list. The meat entries are labeled Ground Meat and Regular Lean Meat.
  • The City Library has shelves by subject and dedicated icons. Market auctions use the current page navigation buttons.

Artwork

  • Pelageya has a complete set of unclothed, secretary, maid, and domina appearances.
  • Wilhelmine has updated full-body art and portraits. The Fabrika scene has an additional image variant.

New

  • You can give your assistant the Special Cooking order. It uses specialty ingredients such as fresh fruit or Agrabah wine to raise a meal’s quality by one grade.
  • Shiru is a new vampire with her own encounter and backstory. Her unclothed, secretary, maid, and domina appearances are all available.
  • New books are available to read in the City Library’s public collection.
  • Semen Addiction is an advanced form of Nymphomania, and Absolute Frigidity is an advanced form of Frigidity.

Fixed

  • Loading an older save restores Shiru’s missing starting trait and shows Semen Addiction in her trait list. Completed Chaotic Essence treatment is preserved.
  • Previously missing requests from customers such as Penelope and Tumak now appear in Objectives.
  • Hawthorne Aliman’s request correctly checks Lana’s handover and allows a retry.
  • Penelope’s previously untranslated lines now appear in Korean.

Known Issues

If you asked Penelope about her request before updating, it may not appear in Objectives automatically. Ask her again or offer a deal to make it appear.
